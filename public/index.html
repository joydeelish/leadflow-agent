Facebook Lead Ads Webhook
Connect your Facebook Lead Ads to automatically send new leads through the LeadFlow pipeline.
Step 1 — Add to your .env
FB_VERIFY_TOKEN=leadflow_verify_2026
You can change this to any string you like.
Step 2 — Deploy your server
Deploy to Railway (or any public URL) so Facebook can reach your webhook. See the deployment guide in README.
Step 3 — Configure in Meta Dashboard
1. Go to developers.facebook.com → Your App → Webhooks
2. Click Add Callback URL
3. Callback URL: https://your-domain.railway.app/api/webhook
4. Verify Token: leadflow_verify_2026
5. Subscribe to leadgen field
6. Also go to WhatsApp → Configuration and subscribe to leads
Step 4 — Map your Ad to a Business
When creating your Facebook Lead Ad, set the Ad Name to your Business ID (e.g. biz_abc123) so LeadFlow routes leads to the right business config.
✓ How it works
Facebook sends a lead → LeadFlow fetches the full data from Meta Graph API → Enrichment Agent pays $0.01 USDC → Scoring Agent pays $0.01 USDC → Qualified leads are sent to your WhatsApp instantly.
