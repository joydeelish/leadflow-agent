How it works after you connect
1
Someone clicks your Facebook Lead Ad
They fill in the instant form — name, email, phone, and any custom questions you set up in Ads Manager.
2
LeadFlow receives the lead instantly
Facebook notifies LeadFlow in real time. No delays, no manual CSV downloads.
3
AI agents qualify the lead
Enrichment Agent researches the person online ($0.01 USDC). Scoring Agent rates them 0–100 using your industry's criteria ($0.01 USDC).
4
Qualified leads land on your WhatsApp
If the score is above your threshold, you get an instant WhatsApp message with the full lead profile, score, and reason. Unqualified leads are filtered out automatically.
